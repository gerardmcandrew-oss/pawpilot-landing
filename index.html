The problem

Your dogs are organised. Are your bookings?

In a smaller daycare, bookings arrive by text, WhatsApp and Instagram. Vaccination dates sit in a folder. Owners message to ask how their dog is getting on, and someone has to stop what they are doing to answer. None of it is difficult. It just never stops.

  • “Another booking has come in by text. Are we already full?”

  • “That vaccination date is somewhere in the paperwork.”

  • “Can someone send an update on how Bella is doing?”

Illustrative examples of everyday daycare questions. They are not quotes from real customers.

Features

One straightforward workspace for the whole day.

Three connected jobs, kept in one place. Every screenshot below comes from the working prototype.

Know when you're full

See bookings, available places and daily capacity in a weekly view.

The alpha prevents bookings beyond the configured capacity and suggests the nearest days with space.

Keep vaccination dates in view

See vaccination warnings and identify records approaching expiry.

In the alpha, an expired record blocks booking for that date and blocks check-in, following the app's implemented rules.

Record the day in one tap

Staff can record what each dog has done without typing.

  • Walked
  • Played
  • Napped

These activities appear in the owner-facing Pupdate preview.

Working demo

See how PawPilot could work in your daycare.

The demo runs as Waggy Tails, the fictional dog daycare from the assignment case. It has three views: Bookings, Today and Pupdate.

  • Bookings: a weekly capacity view with booking checks.
  • Today: check-in, vaccination warnings and one-tap activities.
  • Pupdate: a preview of what an owner would see.

Try booking a dog, checking vaccination eligibility or recording an activity to see how Pupdate works.

The demo uses fictional sample data and saves only in your own browser. It is a working prototype, not a secure live service.
